About

I studied liberal arts and think in systems. Usually I start with a question, then build something to figure out the answer.

A lot of what I make is about taking an abstract topic like economics, money or time and turning it into something a regular person can walk up to and try. At work that might be an investor deck. Outside of work it's been a sorting hat for economic theories and a Lite-Brite board at a civic festival.

I'm interested in financing models that don't depend on an exit, and in policy that puts a price on environmental costs. I'm also studying Zen.
